## Research

### MSc Thesis — *Evaluating the Effect of Germany's Nuclear Phase-Out on Renewable Energy Dispatch*
*LMU Munich · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Marie-Louise Arlt · fully independent*

- **Identification:** April 2023 phase-out as a natural experiment.
- **Data:** ~140,000 observations assembled from ENTSO-E, Network Transparency and futures
  market sources.
- **Methods:** OLS, propensity score matching (implemented from scratch), K-means clustering,
  spatial grid congestion analysis across all four German TSO zones.
- **Central contribution:** distinguished two rival mechanisms — reduced market value of
  renewables vs. increased grid congestion — using interacted regression models rather than
  asserting one.
- **Result:** no aggregate effect on renewable dispatch, but a significant increase in onshore
  wind generation and higher day-ahead prices, implying a net welfare loss.
