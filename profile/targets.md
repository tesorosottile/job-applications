## Aggregators to sweep alongside career pages

- EconJobMarket / INOMICS / RES jobs — best coverage for research economist roles
- EURAXESS — EU research positions
- Absolventa / Stepstone / Indeed (DE) — broad, noisy, use fit_score hard
- Company career pages remain primary; aggregators are supplementary

**Known limitation:** LinkedIn cannot be reliably swept programmatically. If you want LinkedIn
coverage, the practical route is you setting up job alerts there and skimming the email digest
during your Monday triage — 5 minutes, no credits.
