### MSc Economics — Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU)
*2022 – 2024 · Grade 1.69/1.0 (top 25% of cohort)*

**Thesis — *Evaluating the Effect of Germany's Nuclear Phase-Out on Renewable Energy Dispatch***
(Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Marie-Louise Arlt)

- Used the April 2023 phase-out as a natural experiment; ~140,000 observations from ENTSO-E,
  Network Transparency and futures market data.
- Methods: OLS, propensity score matching, K-means clustering, spatial grid congestion
  analysis across all four TSO zones.
- Separated two competing mechanisms — reduced market value of renewables vs. increased grid
  congestion — using interacted regression models.
- Found no aggregate effect on renewable dispatch but a significant rise in onshore wind
  generation and higher day-ahead prices, implying a net welfare loss.
