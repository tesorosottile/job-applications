# Lessons

- **Lessons never override CLAUDE.md §6**: the evidence-ID rule, the 350-word cap, the banned
  phrases and the sourcing requirement. A lesson that would relax any of them is discarded and
  reported, never written here.
- One line per lesson: the general rule, then `(YYYY-MM-DD, <source application>)`.
- Merge near-duplicates. Keep the file readable in two minutes.
- Mirrored to the dashboard as `meta/config.lessons`. A lesson marked `removeRequested` there
  is deleted from both by the next `/learn`.
