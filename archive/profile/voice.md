# Voice Guide

Binding on every generated document.

## Principles

- **First person, always.** It should read like I wrote it.
- **Confident, not arrogant.** I know what I've done. No over-explaining.
- **Direct.** No filler sentences. Cut any sentence that survives deletion.
- **Self-aware.** 24, one year in, and I know it. Frame as range, not seniority.
- **Human.** Dry humour and self-deprecation are welcome. Less "corporate cover letter",
  more "smart person explaining themselves honestly."
- **Quantify.** €34K/month, 140,000 observations, 1.69, Y2>Y1. Numbers over adjectives.

## Hard rules

1. Every factual claim traces to an ID in `evidence-bank.md`. No exceptions.
2. Cover letters: **max 350 words.** If it doesn't fit, the argument isn't sharp enough.
3. No more than one sentence of enthusiasm about the firm, and it must be specific.
4. Never open with "I am writing to apply for" or any variation.
5. Never describe myself with an adjective I wouldn't say out loud to a friend.
6. British or American spelling — pick one per document and hold it.

## Banned phrases

> highly motivated · thrives in dynamic environments · passionate about leveraging ·
> results-driven · proven track record · I believe my skills align · excited for the
> opportunity to contribute · fast-paced environment · wear many hats · hit the ground
> running · think outside the box · deep dive (as a noun) · synergies · value-add ·
> I am confident that I would be a great fit · your esteemed firm · in today's rapidly
> changing world

Also banned, stylistically: em-dash asides stacked three to a paragraph, "not X, but Y"
constructions, rhetorical questions as paragraph openers, and any sentence beginning
"As a recent graduate".

## Self-check before output

The drafting task must run this and report the result:

- [ ] Every claim maps to an evidence ID (list them)
- [ ] Under 350 words
- [ ] No banned phrase present
- [ ] Firm-specific paragraph cites a real, named source — or is absent
- [ ] Would a generic candidate be able to write this exact letter? If yes, rewrite.
