## Research

### *Evaluating the Effect of Germany's Nuclear Phase-Out on Renewable Energy Dispatch*
MSc thesis, LMU Munich · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Marie-Louise Arlt · independent

- Identification via the April 2023 phase-out as a natural experiment.
- ~140,000 observations from ENTSO-E, Network Transparency and futures market data.
- Spatial grid congestion analysis across all four German TSO zones; OLS, propensity score
  matching, K-means clustering.
- Separated reduced market value of renewables from increased grid congestion using interacted
  regressions.
- **Findings:** no aggregate effect on renewable dispatch; significant increase in onshore
  wind generation, most plausibly via reduced congestion in Northwestern Germany; higher
  average day-ahead prices, implying a net welfare loss.
